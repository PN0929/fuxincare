SHARE WITH THE AGENT · 房仲溝通
02 / 04 · BEFORE A VIEWING

先問清楚，再安排看屋

Ask These Questions Before Scheduling a Viewing

海外返台看屋不容易，先請房仲確認這些關鍵問題，避免到了現場才發現房東不接受年長租客。
Screen properties before making the trip. Not every listing will suit a returning retiree.

打給房仲，確認 6 件事 Six questions to ask
房東願意出租給退休／年長租客嗎？Is the landlord open to renting to a retired / older tenant?
房東對獨居或海外子女有哪些顧慮？Any concerns about living alone or family members living abroad?
需要保證人嗎？緊急聯絡人能否由在地協助者擔任？Is a guarantor required? Can a local service contact be listed for emergencies?
月租之外，管理費、水電、其他費用怎麼算？What are the management fees, utilities, and other charges?
簽約要付哪些費用？房仲服務費何時支付？What are the move-in costs and the agent's fee?
能否提供契約先看？修繕、提前退租、續租怎麼寫？Can we review the lease in advance, including repairs, early termination, and renewal?
可以直接使用的說法 A message you can send
您好，我們準備退休回台灣長住，有固定退休收入，也能安排台灣在地聯絡人協助緊急聯繫及日常房屋事務。請問這個物件的房東願意考慮年長租客嗎？

Hello, I'm planning to retire in Taiwan and am looking for a long-term rental. I have a stable retirement income and can arrange a local contact for emergencies and routine housing matters. Would the landlord consider an older tenant?
租金補貼別只問「能不能申請」 Rental subsidy: discuss the practical risk
依法，符合資格的租客申請中央租金補貼不需房東同意，租約也不得禁止申請。但房東可能日後從公益出租人資格、稅單或報稅過程得知；若房東已明確反對，先評估續租風險。
Eligible tenants do not need landlord permission to apply. However, the landlord may later find out through tax or public-welfare-landlord records. Consider renewal risks before proceeding.

03 / 04 · WALK THROUGH THE HOME

看屋時，試著想像十年後的生活

View the Home With Future Needs in Mind

不只看裝潢。從大樓入口走到臥室、浴室與廚房，想想提著東西、身體不舒服，或未來使用助行器、輪椅時，是否仍方便。
Walk through the home as you would use it today—and consider whether it would still work if your mobility changes.

進出大樓 Entrance and common areas
大樓入口、電梯前是否有台階？助行器或輪椅能通過嗎？Any steps at the entrance or elevator? Enough room for a walker or wheelchair?
電梯是否正常？走廊照明、門禁和管理員聯絡方式清楚嗎？Does the elevator work? Are lighting, access and building contacts clear?
垃圾處理，先問清楚 Trash and recycling arrangements
大樓有集中垃圾子母車、垃圾間或回收區嗎？開放時間為何？Is there a shared trash/recycling area or collection container? When is it available?
若沒有集中收集，要用哪一種專用垃圾袋？垃圾車何時、在哪裡停？自己能否方便前往？If there is no shared collection, which designated bags are needed, and where/when does the garbage truck stop? Can you manage this routine?
房屋裡面 Inside the apartment
浴室止滑如何？淋浴區有高門檻嗎？能放洗澡椅、日後加裝扶手嗎？Slippery bathroom floor or high shower step? Space for a shower chair or future grab bars?
主臥床邊有足夠空間站起、移位嗎？若未來需要輪椅，能靠近床邊嗎？Room to get in/out of bed or transfer from a wheelchair if needed?
走道、門框能否讓助行器或輪椅通過？有容易絆倒的門檻或高低差嗎？Wide enough doorways and hallways? Any tripping hazards or floor level changes?
未來若有同住照護者（如外籍家庭看護工），是否有合適的起居與休息空間？If a live-in caregiver is needed later, is there suitable space for daily living and rest?
有漏水、霉味嗎？冷氣、熱水、爐具、插座能現場測試嗎？Any leaks or musty smells? Can you test hot water, AC, cooking appliances and outlets?
周邊生活 Neighborhood
親自走到超市、診所或公車站：路面平整嗎？晚上好走嗎？Walk to shops, a clinic or transit. Are the sidewalks usable, including after dark?
看屋技巧 / Viewing tip：有問題的設備先拍照（取得同意），請房東或房仲用訊息確認何時修好。
With permission, photograph defects and request written confirmation of repairs before move-in.

04 / 04 · BEFORE YOU PAY

簽約前，這幾件事要確認

Before Signing or Paying

確認出租的人有權出租 / Verify the landlord's authority核對屋主身分與房屋所有權資料；若由代理人簽約，確認有屋主授權文件。匯款帳戶與收款人也要核對。
Check the owner's identity and property ownership record. If someone signs for the owner, ask for authorization. Verify the payment recipient and bank account.
押金不得超過兩個月租金 / Deposit: no more than two months' rent月租、押金、管理費、水電及繳款日期，都要寫清楚。
Put rent, deposit, management fees, utilities and due dates in writing.
提前退租與續租 / Early termination and renewal確認多久前需通知、押金如何退還、租約到期後如何處理。
Check notice periods, refunds and what happens when the lease ends.
屋況與修繕 / Property condition and repairs點交時記錄原有損壞，寫清楚哪些設備由誰修、何時修。
Record existing defects and agree who repairs what, and when.
付款有憑據 / Keep payment records保留已簽租約、匯款紀錄、收據與交屋照片。
Keep the signed lease, transfer records, receipts and handover photos.
別為了說服房東而預付一年租金 / Be cautious with large prepayments預繳半年或一年風險較高；若決定預繳，先在契約寫清楚提前退租與剩餘租金如何返還。
Large advance payments increase risk. If you prepay, specify refund terms for unused months.
需要時，可以安排在地協助 Consider a local contact if helpful

如果家人住在海外，可以考慮請台灣親友或照護管家擔任約定的聯絡窗口。福心在服務過程中發現，有明確的在地聯絡安排，常能讓房東比較放心。
A trusted friend, relative or care coordinator in Taiwan may help with communication. In Fuxin's experience, landlords may feel more reassured when a reliable local contact is available.
