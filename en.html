ABOUT FUXIN

Rooted in Taiwan.
Built for families across borders.

Fuxin was founded from an understanding of how challenging distance can make family care. Some parents have always lived in Taiwan; others choose to return to Taiwan for retirement after years abroad. In either case, their children may be building lives overseas while remaining deeply involved in their parents' well-being.

When medical visits, care arrangements, or everyday matters need attention, being far away can make even small tasks difficult to manage. Fuxin aims to be a dependable local partner — helping families organize the practical details while respecting each parent's preferences and independence.

Good support is not just about getting things done. It's about helping families stay connected while allowing older adults to live life on their own terms.
